Individual doll and mask forms face different directions; the featureless spherical/oval heads provide limited facial-axis evidence, so a separate exact 45° yaw for each cannot be measured from this composite. No prominent head is shown as a clear right-profile portrait. The prompt's requested global 45° view is visually plausible, but the per-form orientation evidence is limited.
- **Framing/transparency:** Pass. The silhouette is not clipped. Transparent pixels are present at the corners and around the figure; the one alpha=1 corner pixel and low-alpha red/green fringe at the perimeter are negligible edge artifacts.

This is a direct project-style mismatch, not a transparency or composition issue.
